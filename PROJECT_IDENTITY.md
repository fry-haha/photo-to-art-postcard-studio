# Project identity

The public licenses do not grant permission to use the **Photo To Art Postcard Studio** name, project presentation, or future logo in a way that implies an unofficial fork, commercial product, or derivative project is the original project or is endorsed by its author.

Accurate nominative references such as “made with Photo To Art Postcard Studio” or “based on the Photo To Art Postcard Studio Skill” are welcome when accompanied by a link to the original repository.

Forks and substantial derivatives should use a distinct project name and preserve the notices required by the applicable license.
