# Quality assurance

## Source and truth

- Source remains recognizable.
- Primary subject, location, clothing, and signature objects are accurate.
- No invented narrative objects.
- All readable names, dates, places, lyrics, and quotations are confirmed.
- Unreadable source text is omitted or clearly abstracted, never pseudo-language.

## Portrait and celebrity

- Face remains the same person.
- No malformed hands, limbs, teeth, eyes, hairline, or accessories.
- No duplicate people or stray body parts.
- Sticker edge does not cut through face, hair, or important clothing detail.
- Requested album/song/tour period matches the source photograph and visual cues.
- A requested cutout changes only the background; face, pose, body, and clothing remain source-true.
- No ordinary portrait/detail crop removes the entire head or creates accidental decapitation.
- Each isolated hand shows a legible action, craft, relationship, or character cue.
- Each clothing, jewelry, or accessory crop keeps the featured object complete enough to understand.
- Every sticker fragment passes the independent-meaning test; decorative fragments without a specific function are removed.
- No heavy black frame is placed tightly around or beside an isolated head unless the brief explicitly requests a mourning, ID, or archival treatment.

## Landscape and architecture

- Landmark, roofline, mountain ridge, horizon, canopy, and major branch contours are not accidentally bisected.
- Every landmark sticker that represents the building contains the complete recognizable silhouette; detail studies are explicitly treated as details.
- Rectangular fragments read as intentional windows, viewfinders, or film frames.
- The sticker set uses more than one visual mechanism and does not merely repeat crops of the same view.
- Freer graphic elements are traceable to the source palette, contour, texture, or location; they do not invent an event.
- Arch-window stickers use the arch silhouette plus one edge only, not an arch nested inside an extra rectangle.
- Creek and river stickers preserve the real visible start, full usable course, and true visible endpoint; they are not shortened into an arbitrary decorative blob.

## Visual system

- The back-side hero ground is deliberately selected from a meaningful source color, film black, or film white; it is never an accidental muddy average.
- Accent colors come from the reference or source.
- Postcard and stickers share texture, type, border logic, and decorative language.
- Texture does not obscure the main photograph.
- Pure decorations are few and purposeful.
- Postcard back belongs to the same collection and does not look like an unrelated generic form.
- Every photo-to-paper transition follows subject, structure, light, motion, or material; no generic oval vignette, feathered rectangle, gray halo, or protected-subject cut.
- Every tape or banded-material piece names its material, source evidence, connection points, and spatial task; remove generic office tape, stranded tape, repeated favorite devices, interchangeable overlays, uniform strips, overworked material, unjustified subject crossings, and filler decoration.
- A multi-piece tape ensemble has one shared compositional purpose and distinct roles for every piece; removing any retained piece must weaken attachment, hierarchy, motion, concealment, edge crossing, or depth.
- Tape remains secondary to the subject and preserves primary text, faces, landmark structure, stamp positions, and address lines.
- Source-derived back elements communicate different, identifiable facts; no pair of repeated complete-image thumbnails, same crop at two sizes, meaningless lower-edge strip, or interchangeable abstract decoration.
- At thumbnail size, the back shows a recognizable hero hue or an intentional black/white reason, sufficient contrast for postal lines and type, and a clear visual relationship to the front.
- Archival or museum styling has not collapsed into empty beige when the source provides a stronger product color.

## Production

- Requested front and back exist.
- A usable address area and stamp position exist when a mailable postcard back is requested; a rigid divider and printed lines are optional.
- Transparent stickers have actual alpha.
- Print files contain no preview shadows or perspective.
- Resolution, physical dimensions, bleed, and safe area are checked.
- Draft, revision, final, and production files are not confused.

## Delivery report

State:

- source and rights caveats;
- selected direction, base pair, accents, and texture;
- included deliverables;
- whether production specifications are confirmed or provisional;
- any element omitted because it could not be verified.
